# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Receive technical direction and design guidance; participate in design reviews
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test coverage and edge cases; address quality feedback
- **Project Manager**: Provide estimates and progress updates; flag blockers and dependencies
- **Product Manager**: Clarify acceptance criteria; participate in refinement sessions

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Align on priorities, timelines, and trade-offs; provide stakeholder input
- **Business Analyst**: Define requirements and acceptance criteria with stakeholder input
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Discuss technical feasibility and architecture implications
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define quality standards and acceptance criteria validation

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Collaborate on process facilitation and team health
- **Sponsor/Stakeholder**: Provide executive updates and escalate business-impacting issues
- **Developers**: Track progress, manage dependencies, escalate blockers
- **Product Manager**: Align on priorities, manage scope and timeline trade-offs

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the testing strategy, quality assurance, and acceptance validation for delivered work. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test strategy and test plan
- Develop and execute manual and automated tests
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Identify and triage quality issues (severity, blocker status)
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and edge cases
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and post-release issues
- Enable fast feedback loops through efficient testing processes

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups with development team
- Test plan reviews during sprint planning
- QA sign-off gates in the release process
- Defect reports and trend analysis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, edge cases, and defect triage
- **Product Manager**: Validate acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Project Manager**: Report quality metrics and readiness for release gates
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Review technical approach for testability and test strategy

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They focus on process health and team effectiveness rather than task execution.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Monitor sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and team health
- Shield team from external distractions during the sprint

### Goals
- Maintain a healthy, productive, self-organizing team
- Improve team velocity and cycle time
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (daily, weekly)
- One-on-ones with team members as needed
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Escalations to leadership when blockers require organizational intervention

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Collaborate on process efficiency and team health metrics
- **Developers**: Remove impediments, coach on agile practices
- **Product Manager**: Facilitate backlog refinement and sprint planning alignment
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Ensure technical decisions don't compromise team velocity

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design oversight, and complexity assessment. They ensure architectural consistency and help the team make sound technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical vision and architecture
- Conduct design reviews and provide technical guidance
- Assess technical complexity and identify risks
- Mentor developers on best practices and code quality
- Make or facilitate trade-off decisions between speed and technical debt

### Goals
- Maintain a sound, scalable technical foundation
- Reduce technical debt and security risks
- Enable the team to deliver with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews before implementation
- Code reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk assessment during planning
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and pair programming

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical direction; conduct design and code reviews
- **Project Manager**: Assess technical complexity and identify risks for planning
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Review test strategy for technical soundness
- **Product Manager**: Discuss technical feasibility and architecture implications of features

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive oversight, secure resources, and remove organizational blockers. They represent business priorities and ensure projects align with strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and budgets
- Provide executive air cover and escalation path
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Remove organizational barriers and blockers
- Participate in go/no-go decision gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain strategic alignment and priority
- Enable team success through organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gate reviews (initiation, planning, release)
- Ad-hoc escalation calls for business-impacting issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Receive executive updates; remove organizational blockers
- **Product Manager**: Align on business priorities and strategic objectives
- **Developers**: Provide context on business impact and strategic importance

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify requirements, translate business needs into acceptance criteria, and bridge communication between stakeholders and the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Translate requirements into clear acceptance criteria
- Validate solutions with stakeholders
- Identify scope creep and propose trade-offs
- Document workflows and process changes

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions solve the actual business problem
- Reduce requirements ambiguity and rework
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria reviews with product and development
- Solution validation and UAT support
- Documentation and runbook creation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Translate business needs into prioritized, well-scoped backlog items
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria; gather implementation questions
- **Stakeholders/Sponsor**: Gather business requirements and validate solutions
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test scenarios derived from acceptance criteria
